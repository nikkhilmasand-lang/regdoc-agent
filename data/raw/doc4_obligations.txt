Title: Example Policy Obligations (Sanitized)

1. Access Control
- Systems must enforce role-based access to sensitive data.
- Administrative access shall be limited to authorized personnel only.
- Users should be granted the minimum level of access required to perform their duties.

2. Encryption
- Sensitive data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
- Encryption keys shall be rotated on a defined schedule.
- Weak or deprecated cryptographic algorithms are prohibited.

3. Logging and Monitoring
- Systems must generate audit logs for access to sensitive resources.
- Audit logs shall be retained for at least 12 months.
- Monitoring alerts should be configured for unusual access patterns.

4. Incident Response
- Security incidents must be reported within 24 hours of detection.
- A documented incident response process shall be maintained and reviewed periodically.
- Post-incident reviews should identify corrective actions and owners.
