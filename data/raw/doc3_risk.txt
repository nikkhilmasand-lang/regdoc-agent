Title: Risk Identification and Mitigation Overview

Risk management involves identifying potential events or conditions that could negatively impact systems, users, or organizational objectives. These risks may be technical, operational, legal, or reputational in nature. Early identification allows teams to prioritize risks based on likelihood and potential impact.

Once identified, risks are typically evaluated and categorized. Mitigation strategies may include preventive controls, monitoring mechanisms, or contingency plans. For example, technical risks might be addressed through redundancy and testing, while operational risks could be reduced through process improvements and training.

Risk management is an ongoing process rather than a one-time activity. As systems evolve and external conditions change, new risks may emerge while existing risks may diminish. Regular reviews, incident retrospectives, and updates to risk registers help ensure that mitigation efforts remain effective and aligned with current realities.
